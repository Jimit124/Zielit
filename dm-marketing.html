Capability · DM Marketing

Direct-message marketing that sounds like a person

Direct-message outreach on the platforms your audience already scrolls — X, LinkedIn, TikTok, and beyond, written to sound like a person.

In shortZielit runs direct-message outreach on social platforms: finding the right people, writing messages that read like a human wrote them, following up, and logging replies back to the record — within each platform's rules and your privacy assessment.

How it runs: find → message → follow up

  1. Find — identify the people worth reaching on the platform where they already spend time.
  2. Message — short, specific, written to sound like a person, not a sequence.
  3. Follow up — timed follow-ups, with replies and opt-outs logged back to the record.

Same audience, every channel

Your list is built once and used consistently across email, phone and DM — no duplicated or conflicting outreach. A person who opts out in one channel shouldn't be chased in another.

Rules that apply

Each platform sets its own terms for messaging and automation, and privacy laws still apply to the personal data used to target and track outreach. We plan DM programs inside both.

Frequently asked questions

Which platforms do you run DM outreach on?

X, LinkedIn, TikTok and other platforms where your audience is active.

Is DM marketing covered by privacy law?

The personal data used to select, message and track people is generally covered by applicable privacy laws, and each platform's own terms also govern messaging and automation.

Can DM run alongside email and phone?

Yes. The audience is built once and used consistently across channels so outreach isn't duplicated or conflicting.
