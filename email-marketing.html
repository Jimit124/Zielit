Capability · Email Marketing

Email marketing, consent-checked before every send

Lists built and segmented properly, campaigns designed to be opened, and subject lines tested before the full send goes out.

In shortZielit plans, builds and sends email campaigns from your customer data. Every row is validated and de-duplicated, every contact is tracked as opted in, opted out or unknown, and nothing is emailed on consent that hasn't been confirmed.

From your data to a ready-to-send audience

  1. Send your data — drop a CSV into a secure SFTP folder or push records through our API, whichever fits how your systems already export.
  2. We validate and load it — every row is checked against our format, bad rows are flagged (not silently dropped), and records are de-duplicated into your contact base.
  3. We find your audience — rule-based segments on engagement, industry, geography and consent status narrow the list to people worth reaching.
  4. You launch — a named, reusable segment comes back to you, and it stays current as new data comes in.

The email rules we build in

US commercial email is governed by CAN-SPAM, which requires accurate header information and subject lines, identification of the message as an ad where applicable, a valid physical postal address, a clear way to opt out, and honoring opt-outs within 10 business days. Audiences in other places can bring stricter, opt-in rules (for example GDPR in the EU). We apply the rules that fit your audience; see TCPA vs. CAN-SPAM for a side-by-side.

How the campaign runs

Discover, strategize, execute, optimize: we agree a number to hit before anything goes out, then report against it and adjust the plan before you have to ask.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need opt-in consent to send marketing email in the US?

CAN-SPAM does not require prior opt-in for commercial email, but it does require a working opt-out, honoring opt-outs within 10 business days, a valid physical postal address and non-deceptive headers and subject lines. Other laws, and many email platforms, can impose stricter requirements depending on the audience.

How do you handle contacts with unknown consent status?

They're tracked as unknown from the moment they're loaded and are not emailed until consent is confirmed.

How do I send you my data?

Through a secure SFTP folder (CSV) or via API — whichever already fits how your systems export data.

Can I hire you for email only?

Yes. Each channel is available on its own.
