Email Marketing · How it works

From your customer data to a ready-to-send audience.

Send us what you have, however it already leaves your systems. We handle the validation, loading, and targeting — you get back a list built to hit the number you set.

Send data via
SFTP upload API connection
01

Send your data

Drop a CSV into your secure SFTP folder, or push records through our API — whichever already fits how your systems export data.

02

We validate & load it

Every row is checked against our format, bad rows are flagged (not silently dropped), and records are de-duplicated into your contact base.

03

We find your audience

Rule-based segments — engagement, industry, geography, consent status — narrow the list to the people actually worth reaching.

04

You launch

A named, reusable segment comes back to you, ready for the campaign — and it stays current as new data comes in.

Nothing gets emailed on consent we haven't confirmed — every contact is tracked as opted in, opted out, or unknown from the moment it's loaded.

AI Governance

AI belongs in your funnel. It also belongs on a leash.

We enable AI in marketing — and we govern it, so a drafting tool never quietly becomes a decision-maker. Every AI touchpoint we set up ships with a record of what it's allowed to do, who signs off on it, and how you'd explain it to a regulator or a client.

Mapped to GDPR, CCPA, and the EU AI Act

Transparency

Every AI-assisted step is logged and disclosed — no black-box copy or scoring your team can't explain.

Human oversight

A person reviews anything AI drafts or scores before it reaches a customer — always, no exceptions.

Data stewardship

AI tools only ever see the data your privacy assessment says they're allowed to — nothing more.

Compliance mapping

Every AI use case is mapped to the regulation it touches, kept current as the rules change.
