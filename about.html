Who we are

Data only matters once it changes what you do next.

Zielit is a data-driven marketing practice built around one idea: your customer data should decide the channel, the message, and the moment — not the other way around. We plan, run, and measure email, phone, and direct-message programs end to end, with a privacy assessment behind every list and AI enabled only where it earns its place in the funnel.
